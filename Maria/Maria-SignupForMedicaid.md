## What's Happening

Maria is living with her parents in Albany. She enrolls in Medicaid and is assigned CIN **A123B456**. This CIN is permanent — it will stay with Maria for life regardless of where she moves, what name she uses, or what provider she visits.

The MPI creates a record and assigns VeratoLinkID **124252...** — this is the system's way of saying "this is one person."

At this point, everything is clean: one person, one record, one CIN.
