Maria's Story

Maria is a Medicaid member who is HIV-positive and pregnant. But no system can see all three facts are about the same person — because the feeds that carry her HIV result and pregnancy don't include her CIN.

1 Maria Signs Up for Medicaid (2019)

Maria lives with her parents in Albany. She enrolls in Medicaid and is assigned CIN A123B456.

4 The System Fails Maria

Medicaid has a program for pregnant HIV-positive members — coordinated prenatal care, antiretroviral support, prevention of mother-to-child transmission.

The program queries: "Show me members who are Medicaid-enrolled AND HIV-positive AND pregnant."

VeratoLinkIDWhat the System KnowsWhat's Missing
124252...Maria Alvarez, Albany — Medicaid memberNo HIV, no pregnancy
23456...Maria Alvarez, Utica — HIV-positiveNo Medicaid, no pregnancy
78910...Maria Rodriguez, Whitesborough — PregnantNo Medicaid, no HIV
Maria is not identified. No outreach is triggered. She navigates a high-risk pregnancy with HIV without the support Medicaid designed for her.

5 How CIN Fixes This

Now replay the same story — but this time, every feed includes Maria's CIN.

One CIN. One person. Instant match. Despite different names, addresses, MRNs, and providers — CIN links all three records deterministically. The MPI resolves them to a single identity. Outreach fires. Maria gets help.
