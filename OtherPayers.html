What About Other Payer IDs?

A natural question. If adding CIN to the MPI improves matching for Medicaid members, shouldn't we do the same for commercial payers — UnitedHealthcare subscriber IDs, Aetna member numbers, CDPHP IDs?

Yes. Eventually. But we're starting with Medicaid for clear reasons.

Why Medicaid first

  • We are predominantly funded by Medicaid. The SHIN-NY exists in large part to serve this population. They come first.
  • Medicaid members are the most vulnerable. They move more frequently, change providers more often, and have the most to gain from better matching — as Maria's story shows.
  • CIN is a single, statewide identifier. Every Medicaid member in New York gets one CIN from one source. Commercial payers each have their own schemes — that's more complex to implement.
  • We want to prove the model first. Getting CIN right will teach us what works, what challenges arise, and how to design the approach for other payer IDs.

The roadmap

Now

Medicaid CIN

Get CIN into QE infrastructure, convey to statewide MPI. Prove the model. Measure improvement in matching for Medicaid members.

Next

Learn and refine

Understand what worked, what didn't. Document the integration patterns. Identify what's reusable for other payer identifiers.

Future

Other payer IDs

Apply the lessons from CIN to commercial payer member IDs. The infrastructure and patterns we build now will inform the design for those identifiers.

What CIN teaches us about other payer IDs

The CIN work will answer questions that apply to any payer identifier:

  • How do QEs best capture payer IDs from clinical feeds?
  • How does the MPI weight a payer ID in the match algorithm?
  • What happens when a payer ID has errors or duplicates?
  • What's the measurable improvement in match rates?
  • What's the QE level of effort and how is it funded?

We believe CIN will directly inform the design for other payers. Get this right, and scaling to commercial payer IDs becomes a known problem rather than an unknown one.

Medicaid first. Get 100% through their members. Then expand.

We owe it to our primary funder and to the population we exist to serve. Once CIN is proven, the path to other payer IDs will be clear — and built on real experience, not guesswork.
